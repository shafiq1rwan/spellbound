Spellbound Arena · concept art for review

Chapter Bosses

One new boss for each chapter on the roadmap. They're drawn at the game's own pixel size (96×96, like the Ancient Guardian) and shown enlarged. Every animation plays at its in-game timing.

Chapter 1 · Crypt · waves 1–10 · current

Ancient Guardian

The boss in the game today, shown at the same scale for comparison. Today it returns at waves 20 and 30; with chapters, each one gets its own boss.

Chapter 2 · Frozen Ruins · waves 11–20

Rime Queen

A hooded wraith of ice under a crystal crown. Her robe trails into frost, her claws float at her sides, and three ice shards circle her. She fights from range, filling the arena with ice the player has to break.

Weak to FireShrugs off Ice NovaProposed

Icicle Rain

Raises her arms and her claws charge with frost, then icicles fall on marked spots. Shoot them before they land.

Frost Slam

Drives her claws into the ground and sends a ring of ice shards outward. Fireballs melt the shards.

Icicle

The falling projectile, with a glint running down its edge.

Hurt

Flashes white and recoils, and her eyes go dark.

Death

Shatters outward into ice chips that fall and fade.

Chapter 3 · Ember Wastes · waves 21–30

Cinder Maw

A horned obsidian skull with magma in its cracks, a crown of fire and lava dripping from its jaw. Its big attack is telegraphed by the mouth: when the jaw starts to glow, fire is coming.

Weak to IceIce Nova snuffs the breathProposed

Wind-up

The jaw creaks open, the throat glows and the flames grow. This is the warning, and an Ice Nova cast now cancels the breath.

Fire Breath

A jet of fire pours down toward the wizard.

Magma Rock

Spat out in arcs between breaths. Any spell breaks it in the air.

Hurt

Flashes and jolts back, and its eyes dim.

Death

The fire goes out and the skull crumbles to ash from the top down.

Chapter 4 · Astral Void · waves 31–40

Void Weaver

A drifting cosmic jellyfish. Its bell holds a field of stars, one eye watches from the middle, and its tendrils glow at the tips. It doesn't stay put: it blinks across the arena, so the player has to keep re-aiming.

Weak to LightningLightning stuns it mid-chargeProposed

Gathering

The eye opens wide and light gathers under the bell.

Star Volley

The tendrils flare out and it fires a volley of homing star bolts.

Blink

Collapses into a point of light and reforms somewhere else in the arena.

Star Bolt

A spinning star that homes in slowly. Arcane missiles intercept it.

Hurt

Flashes and the eye squeezes shut.

Death

Implodes into a star that flares once and goes out.

Names, attacks and weaknesses are proposals to react to, not decisions. Each attack is designed so a rooted wizard can answer it with a spell.

Action (what the game is about): above the staff, a large glowing orange triangle rune is being drawn: two

Composition: three readable shapes only: the big wizard (bottom-left/center), the orange triangle + fireball

Essential elements inside the central 80% so corner rounding doesn't clip them. Check it at 94px: the

Text: none.
Constraints: square, at least 1024x1024, opaque, edge-to-edge. No words, title, letters, numbers, logo,
